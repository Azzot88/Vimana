The problem

Urgent, fragile and important things can't wait for cargo.

Documents, medicine, spare parts and gifts for family left behind need to move in days — intact and tracked. People already solve this informally: they hand parcels to travelers in chat groups. But there is no verified trust, no payment rail, and no record if something goes wrong. Running an office of operators to coordinate it is uneconomical, so the coordination problem stays unsolved.

Validation

The demand is proven — not hypothetical.

The market is already moving large volumes through chat groups. PeerFlew doesn't create the behavior; it makes it safe and accountable.

EXISTING MARKET

Thousands already paying

Travel-courier chats run tens of thousands of members. Senders and carriers transact informally every day, at a price they accept.

FOUNDER PROOF

Lived the use case

The founder imported ~30 boxes of premium headphones every two weeks from China this way to stock an e-commerce store — and captured real market share with it.

EARLY TRACTION

First deliveries done

PeerFlew has already run its first deliveries by hand, validating the flow before a line of product code was written.

How it works

Senders and carriers run the whole trip themselves.

Carriers publish their own flight schedule and accept permitted goods. PeerFlew sits underneath as the trust and payment layer.

Gate 01
01

Post

Sender lists a parcel, route and value. A carrier with spare suitcase space sees it.

Gate 02
02

Match

A verified traveler on that route accepts. Escrow / collateral is set for high-value cargo.

Gate 03
03

Hand-off

Pickup is documented with photos of both parties and the contents in a private, undeletable chat.

Gate 04
04

Release

Receipt is photographed; escrow releases. Payment happens on-platform or in person.

The trust layer

Three pillars that a chat group can't offer.

This is the product. Everything else is a marketplace; this is why people switch to PeerFlew.

Peer-verified carriers

Travelers are verified by other users, building a reputation that follows them from trip to trip.

Tamper-proof record

Document photos live in a private chat that cannot be deleted. Hand-off and receipt photos capture both sender and carrier.

Escrow-linked collateral

For valuable cargo, a carrier deposit and escrow in BTC / USDT are logically tied to the transaction and released on confirmed receipt.

  • Carriers verified by other users
  • Document photos kept in a private chat
  • Chat cannot be deleted by either party
  • Hand-off photo with sender + carrier
  • Receipt photo on delivery
  • Escrow transaction linked to high-value cargo

Business model

Three revenue streams, one secure transaction.

Primary · at launch

Escrow fee

A fee on every secure (escrowed) transaction. This is the core revenue stream from day one — people pay for safety.

Secondary

Card payment processing

A percentage on funds moved through the platform via integrated card systems.

Premium

Storage & records

Subscription for document storage and accounting — built on the founder's payment-integrated product experience.
